micromachee — a 128x128, eight-colour console that lives in your bar

PLAYING
  micromachee status          one line of JSON for the bar
  micromachee list            the shelf, as JSON
  micromachee play <id>       run a cart (frames on stdout, buttons on stdin)
  micromachee sync            fetch carts from the catalog
  micromachee doctor          where everything lives

MAKING A GAME
  micromachee new "<name>"    write a starting cart that already plays
  micromachee check <file>    does it load, and survive a minute of play?
  micromachee shot <file> [--frames N] [--hold 2,4] [-o out.png]
                              look at it, with buttons held down

A cart is one Lua file, at most 24K. It needs _draw(); _init() and _update()
are optional. 30 frames a second.

  cls(c) pset(x,y,c) pget(x,y) rect(x,y,w,h,c) rectb(x,y,w,h,c)
  line(x0,y0,x1,y1,c) circ(x,y,r,c) circb(x,y,r,c) print(text,x,y,c)
  btn(i) btnp(i) t() rnd(n) flr(n) mid(lo,v,hi) score(n)

Colours 0-7: black navy red orange yellow green blue white
Buttons 0-5: left right up down O(z) X(x)
